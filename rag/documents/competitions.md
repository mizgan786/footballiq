# Top 5 European Leagues and Competitions

FootballIQ's dataset covers Europe's "top five" domestic leagues, widely
considered the strongest in club football by revenue, broadcast reach,
and player quality:

- **Premier League** (England) - 20 clubs, widely regarded as the
  richest league, which is reflected in generally higher market values
  for comparable statistical output versus the other four leagues.
- **La Liga** (Spain) - 20 clubs, historically home to some of the
  world's most decorated clubs.
- **Bundesliga** (Germany) - 18 clubs, known for developing young
  talent and high-intensity attacking football.
- **Serie A** (Italy) - 20 clubs, historically known for tactical and
  defensive sophistication.
- **Ligue 1** (France) - 18 clubs, often a proving ground for young
  players before moves to bigger leagues.

## Promotion and Relegation
Each of these leagues uses a promotion/relegation system with a
second-tier domestic league: the bottom few clubs each season are
relegated down, and the top clubs from the tier below are promoted up.

## European Competitions
Alongside their domestic leagues, top clubs also compete in UEFA club
competitions (the Champions League, Europa League, and Conference
League), qualification for which is determined mainly by domestic
league position. FootballIQ's player statistics are drawn only from
domestic league matches, not from these European competitions, so a
player's appearance and goal counts in the app reflect league form
specifically.

## Transfer Windows
Clubs can generally only buy and sell players during two transfer
windows each season: a longer summer window between seasons, and a
shorter winter window partway through the season. Market value tends
to be most volatile around these windows as transfer speculation and
completed deals both move perception of a player's worth.
